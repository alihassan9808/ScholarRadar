Malaysia MTCP Scholarship 2026/27: eligibility, funding and deadline

Applications closed. The 2026 MTCP application window ran from 4 May to 12 June 2026. The official application portal currently states that submissions are closed. A new 2027 application deadline has not been announced in the sources checked. Do not submit an application based on last year's dates.

At a glance

  • Provider: Ministry of Foreign Affairs Malaysia, Malaysian Technical Cooperation Programme (MTCP).
  • Country and institution: Malaysia; participating universities listed by the official MTCP programme (verify university eligibility in the relevant call).
  • Level: Full-time Master's, not undergraduate or PhD.
  • Eligible nationalities: MTCP recipient countries only. Check the official recipient-country list; eligibility is not universal.
  • Age: Not over 45 years at application in the 2026 guidelines.
  • Academic threshold: At least second-class upper honours or undergraduate CGPA 3.0 or equivalent (2026 rules).
  • English: IELTS 6.0, TOEFL iBT 60, or evidence that a previous degree was taught in English, according to the 2026 guidelines. A waiver is not automatic; admission requirements can differ.
  • Duration: 24 months for coursework or 36 months for mixed-mode/research according to the official 2026 FAQ.
  • Start: September/October 2026 intake for the closed cycle. Future start dates unknown.
  • Application deadline/timezone: 12 June 2026, now expired; exact cutoff time not specified in the reviewed guidelines.

Funding: what is and is not covered

The 2026 guidelines specify tuition fees paid to the university, a monthly allowance and an economy-class flight to Malaysia plus a return ticket after completion. The exact monthly allowance amount is not established from the reviewed guidelines and is intentionally not stated here. The 2026 FAQ says university application fees are paid by applicants, while visa costs and related expenses are also not covered. The applicant pays for the required medical examination. Check whether a future cycle changes these terms.

Study fields

2026 priority fields included information and communication technology, economy and trade, STEM, food security and agriculture technology, Islamic finance, law, public administration/policy, and sustainable and environmental science. Nursing, medicine and clinical pharmacy were excluded. A programme must also meet participating-university requirements.

Documents and how to apply

  1. Check the official MTCP portal for an announced application cycle and participating universities.
  2. Apply to a participating Malaysian university and obtain an offer. The 2026 FAQ allowed conditional offers or proof of university application at submission, but a valid full offer was needed for award consideration.
  3. Prepare passport information page (12 months validity under the 2026 rules), undergraduate transcript and grading explanation where needed, English evidence or official medium-of-instruction letter, CV, medical report, two referee letters and university offer/application evidence.
  4. When the next call opens, register and submit through the official MTCP scholarship application system; monitor it for updates. Do not use unofficial agents.

Specific application portal: MTCP application system (currently closed). A separate scholarship application fee was not identified in the reviewed guidelines; university application and visa costs are applicant expenses.

MTCP versus MIS

MTCP and Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) are different government programmes. MTCP is administered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs; MIS is administered by the Ministry of Higher Education. MTCP's 2026 call was Master's only, while MIS can have different degree levels and requirements. See the MIS guide before comparing eligibility.

Frequently asked questions

Is MTCP open for 2027 applications?

No 2027 opening was verified on 23 November 2026. The official submission system says closed.

Can I apply without IELTS?

The 2026 guidelines allowed evidence of an earlier degree taught in English instead of IELTS/TOEFL. A university may impose additional admission conditions.

Can PhD applicants apply?

Not under the 2026 MTCP scholarship call, which was restricted to Master's programmes.

Is it completely free to apply?

Do not assume so: university application fees and visa costs were not covered by the 2026 scholarship.
