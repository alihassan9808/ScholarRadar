Singapore research scholarships for 2027: NUS, NTU and A*STAR

Looking for a funded research degree or attachment in Singapore? These are four distinct funding routes. For academic year 2027–28, NTU NPGS closes 31 December 2026 (NIE: 30 December). The NUS College of Design and Engineering closes 15 January 2027 for international applicants to its August 2027 research intake. Other programmes and faculties may use different dates.

1. NUS ASEAN Research Scholarship — PhD

Provider: National University of Singapore (NUS), Singapore. Nationality: ASEAN citizens except Singaporeans. Level: PhD research. Academic minimum: an undergraduate degree equivalent to at least Second Class Upper / Honours (Distinction), subject to programme admission. Disciplines: eligible NUS graduate research programmes; faculty-specific requirements apply.

Funding: full tuition waiver and SGD 3,000 monthly for eligible international ASEAN PhD students, with an additional SGD 500 per month after passing the PhD qualifying examination, according to NUS rates effective 1 January 2026. Duration: typically up to four years, subject to satisfactory progress and renewal. Graduate assistantship duties apply; NUS states no specific service bond.

Deadline: varies by faculty for August and January admissions; no single 2027 closing date is published on the scholarship page. Apply: submit a graduate admission application via NUS GradApp and complete scholarship fields as instructed. English scores, age restrictions, exact document checklist, application fee and timezone: not specified centrally on the scholarship page; verify with your chosen department and admissions portal.

Official NUS ASEAN scholarship requirements and application instructions

2. NUS Research Scholarship

Eligibility: Open to applicants of all nationalities for eligible PhD and research master's programmes. NUS lists a bachelor's degree equivalent to at least Second Class Honours (Upper); individual programmes have separate admission requirements.

Funding: Full tuition waiver and S$3,000 per month for international PhD students at the published January 2026 rate. The initial award is renewable subject to satisfactory progress, up to four years for PhD candidates. International scholars must meet the Graduate Assistantship Programme requirements.

Application: Request scholarship consideration in the graduate admissions application. For the College of Design and Engineering (CDE), the August 2027 international deadline is 15 January 2027, with or without a scholarship request. Check the portal for the closing hour. CDE charges a non-refundable S$50 application fee per programme; other faculties set their own admission dates and requirements.

Official NUS Research Scholarship · CDE application dates and instructions

3. NTU Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship — PhD

Provider: Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. Nationality: all nationalities. Level: full-time PhD. Academic requirement: Bachelor's Honours (Highest Distinction) or equivalent, with evidence of expected results for final-year candidates. The award is competitive and assesses academic, research and leadership potential. Discipline: subject to eligible NTU PhD admission.

Funding: Annual tuition and S$4,300 per month for international students at the published January 2026 rate. Graduate assistantship duties apply to international students. Duration: initially one year, renewable subject to progress, up to four years. Bond: none.

AY 2027–28 award window: 1 November to 31 December 2026 (NIE: 2 November to 30 December 2026). Exact closing hours are not published on the award page. Application: The award is nomination-based. The checklist includes a research proposal and two referee reports. Check the separate NTU admissions guide for your school's admission deadline, intake, language requirements and application fee.

Official NTU Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship

4. A*STAR Research Attachment Programme (ARAP)

Provider: Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR), Singapore. Type: research attachment, not a standalone degree scholarship. Eligible applicants: overseas-based postgraduate/PhD students jointly nominated by home-university and A*STAR supervisors. Applicants should normally hold at least a Second Class Upper-equivalent Bachelor's degree and demonstrate strong research potential. Research discipline: depends on the agreed A*STAR-hosted project.

Duration: usually one to two years. Support: monthly stipend, housing allowance, one-time IT, airfare and settling-in allowances, visa costs, medical insurance and conference allowance; amounts are not specified in the reviewed programme summary.

Published recurring nomination dates: 1 June and 1 December. As of 25 November 2026, the next published calendar date is 1 December 2026, but eligibility for that particular nomination round and exact cutoff timezone must be reconfirmed by the supervisors. How to apply: coordinated nomination through A*STAR and the home institution, rather than an independent student application. Age restriction, language threshold, exact documents and fees: not confirmed from the official summary. A*STAR says attachments may start around six months after award.

Official A*STAR ARAP programme and nomination process

Which programme should you investigate?

Consider the NUS Research Scholarship regardless of nationality, or compare it with the NUS ASEAN award if you qualify for both. Consider NTU NPGS if you have an exceptional undergraduate record and want to enter an NTU PhD. Consider ARAP if you are already enrolled in a postgraduate or PhD programme abroad and your supervisors can arrange a joint research attachment. Do not confuse ARAP with SINGA, which is a separate Singapore International Graduate Award.

For SINGA, consult the official A*STAR programme page. Its exact 2027 deadline and requirements were not independently verified during this update, so no opening status is claimed here.

Application preparation

Before applying, verify the current departmental call, your citizenship eligibility, qualification equivalence, supervisor or research fit, language requirements, documents, fees, deadline with timezone, scholarship terms and admission start date. Typical documents may include transcripts, CV, research statement and referees, but only the actual provider checklist is authoritative. Scholarship funding does not guarantee admission or an award.

Frequently asked questions

Which 2027 deadlines are confirmed?

The published NTU NPGS window closes 31 December 2026 (30 December for NIE). NUS CDE international applications for August 2027 close 15 January 2027; check separately for other faculties. ARAP publishes recurring nomination dates and requires supervisor coordination.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Do not assume a waiver. Check the language policy of the relevant graduate programme and applicant category.

Does ARAP award a PhD degree?

No. It supports a research attachment for students enrolled elsewhere.
