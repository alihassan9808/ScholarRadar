Singapore research scholarships for 2027: NUS, NTU and A*STAR

Looking for a funded research degree or attachment in Singapore? These are three distinct official programmes, not one common application. The information below is for planning a 2027 application; a 2027 intake deadline is not independently confirmed for any programme here. Always use the linked official provider page for the active intake.

1. NUS ASEAN Research Scholarship — PhD

Provider: National University of Singapore (NUS), Singapore. Nationality: ASEAN citizens except Singaporeans. Level: PhD research. Academic minimum: an undergraduate degree equivalent to at least Second Class Upper / Honours (Distinction), subject to programme admission. Disciplines: eligible NUS graduate research programmes; faculty-specific requirements apply.

Funding: full tuition waiver and SGD 3,000 monthly for eligible international ASEAN PhD students, with an additional SGD 500 per month after passing the PhD qualifying examination, according to NUS rates effective 1 January 2026. Duration: typically up to four years, subject to satisfactory progress and renewal. Graduate assistantship duties apply; NUS states no specific service bond.

Deadline: varies by faculty for August and January admissions; no single 2027 closing date is published on the scholarship page. Apply: submit a graduate admission application via NUS GradApp and complete scholarship fields as instructed. English scores, age restrictions, exact document checklist, application fee and timezone: not specified centrally on the scholarship page; verify with your chosen department and admissions portal.

Official NUS ASEAN scholarship requirements and application instructions

2. NTU Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship — PhD

Provider: Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. Nationality: all nationalities. Level: full-time PhD. Academic requirement: Bachelor's Honours (Highest Distinction) or equivalent, with evidence of expected results for final-year candidates. The award is competitive and assesses academic, research and leadership potential. Discipline: subject to eligible NTU PhD admission.

Funding: scholarship benefits and stipend amounts depend on the current official award schedule and applicant category; consult the NTU page rather than assuming a fixed rate. Duration: initially one year, renewable subject to progress, up to four years. Bond: none.

Deadline and start date: programme and award application dates must be confirmed for the intended 2027 intake. Application: follow NTU's official graduate admissions and scholarship instructions. Age limit, language test, application fee, documents and deadline timezone: check the current school-specific admission instructions; not established from the central scholarship summary.

Official NTU Nanyang President's Graduate Scholarship

3. A*STAR Research Attachment Programme (ARAP)

Provider: Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A*STAR), Singapore. Type: research attachment, not a standalone degree scholarship. Eligible applicants: overseas-based postgraduate/PhD students jointly nominated by home-university and A*STAR supervisors. Applicants should normally hold at least a Second Class Upper-equivalent Bachelor's degree and demonstrate strong research potential. Research discipline: depends on the agreed A*STAR-hosted project.

Duration: usually one to two years. Support: monthly stipend, housing allowance, one-time IT, airfare and settling-in allowances, visa costs, medical insurance and conference allowance; amounts are not specified in the reviewed programme summary.

Published recurring nomination dates: 1 June and 1 December. As of 24 November 2026, the next published calendar date is 1 December 2026, but eligibility for that particular nomination round and exact cutoff timezone must be reconfirmed by the supervisors. How to apply: coordinated nomination through A*STAR and the home institution, rather than an independent student application. Age restriction, language threshold, exact documents and fees: not confirmed from the official summary. A*STAR says attachments may start around six months after award.

Official A*STAR ARAP programme and nomination process

Which programme should you investigate?

Choose NUS ASEAN if you are an eligible ASEAN national seeking a research PhD. Consider NTU NPGS if you have an exceptional undergraduate record and want to enter an NTU PhD. Consider ARAP if you are already enrolled in a postgraduate or PhD programme abroad and your supervisors can arrange a joint research attachment. Do not confuse ARAP with SINGA, which is a separate Singapore International Graduate Award.

For SINGA, consult the official A*STAR programme page. Its exact 2027 deadline and requirements were not independently verified during this update, so no opening status is claimed here.

Application preparation

Before applying, verify the current departmental call, your citizenship eligibility, qualification equivalence, supervisor or research fit, language requirements, documents, fees, deadline with timezone, scholarship terms and admission start date. Typical documents may include transcripts, CV, research statement and referees, but only the actual provider checklist is authoritative. Scholarship funding does not guarantee admission or an award.

Frequently asked questions

Are these three programmes currently accepting applications?

Open-call status is not confirmed here. NUS and NTU dates depend on admissions; ARAP publishes recurring nomination dates but requires supervisor coordination.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Do not assume a waiver. Check the language policy of the relevant graduate programme and applicant category.

Does ARAP award a PhD degree?

No. It supports a research attachment for students enrolled elsewhere.
