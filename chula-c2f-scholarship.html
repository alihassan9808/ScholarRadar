Funding and benefits

Research-oriented awards may include tuition support and living allowances. Housing support and research-output conditions depend on the scheme. Master's and doctoral schemes are listed by university faculties.

Eligibility and application route

Identify the relevant high-potential master's or doctoral scheme and contact a prospective supervisor. Faculty sponsorship may be required. Confirm program admission, research expectations, publication targets and any retention-payment conditions before accepting.

Document preparation

Prepare academic records, research proposal, CV and supervisor support as required by the applicable call. Ask about publication milestones and funding duration.
