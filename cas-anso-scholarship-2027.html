CAS-ANSO Scholarship 2027: choose UCAS or USTC

Start with the department or lab you want, then choose its application portal. This award supports international graduate study at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), including eligible CAS institutes, or the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

One host only: Applying for CAS-ANSO through both UCAS and USTC disqualifies you. The central deadline for both applicants and referees is 28 February 2027, Beijing time (UTC+8). The call does not specify an hour. Your host institute may close earlier.

Official sources checked 27 November 2026. Read the official 2027 call.

What the award covers

Master’s

CNY 3,000/month for up to 36 months. Applicants must have been born on or after 1 January 1997.

PhD

CNY 6,000 or 7,000/month for up to 48 months. The higher rate depends on passing the university’s qualifying test. Applicants must have been born on or after 1 January 1992.

Both awards waive tuition and the application fee, and include health insurance. The call lists one inbound trip from the awardee’s home country and a one-time visa or residence permit allowance under sponsor rules. If you are in China when you apply, you are ineligible for the travel subsidy. Housing is not listed as a separate benefit, so check local rent and dorm availability before comparing offers.

The award is for non-Chinese citizens who meet their host’s degree and language requirements. You cannot apply at a level you are already studying at in China. Continuation is subject to annual assessments and award terms. Compare the stipend with actual living costs.

Which route fits your application?

UCAS: choose a training institute

Start here if your department or research group is part of UCAS or a participating CAS institute. Check the specific lab, language of instruction and local deadline. The central UCAS guide does not require prior supervisor approval, but ask the institute about any local process.

USTC: choose a university program

Start here if your preferred department is at USTC. Its 2027 graduate guides list English- and Chinese-taught programs and say you need not contact a supervisor before applying. The general USTC admission window is not the CAS-ANSO deadline.

USTC application portal · Master’s guide · PhD guide

Check the earlier cutoff: For example, the CAS Institute of Hydrobiology lists a 31 January 2027, noon Beijing time, local deadline. That date is for that institute, not all of UCAS. If your host gives a separate deadline, work to the earlier date and confirm the referee timing with the host.

Prepare the documents before opening the portal

  1. Confirm the program and language. The hosts offer fields such as chemistry, physics and engineering; available programs differ. For English-taught study, check which proof of English the department accepts. UCAS typically asks for HSK 5 with at least 180 for Chinese-taught study; USTC lists HSK 4 or higher for its Chinese-taught graduate programs. Read the route’s exemptions and requirements.
  2. Gather passport, degree and transcript records. UCAS accepts current official transcripts and a university letter with the expected graduation date if you are due to graduate before September 2027; final records are due before registration. USTC’s provisional route calls for expected degree completion by July 2027 and specifies notarized academic documents. Do not assume one host’s document rules apply to the other.
  3. Give two academic referees time. Both universities request letters from professors or associate professors. UCAS requires institutional letterhead, signature and date, and the proposed UCAS supervisor cannot serve as a referee. Verify completion in the portal before the cutoff.
  4. Prepare a tailored application. UCAS lists a CV, motivation letter and published-paper excerpts if available. USTC asks for a CV, a study or research proposal of at least 1,000 words, a recent medical form and a criminal record clearance. Check the official checklist for translation, passport validity and formatting.
  5. Select CAS-ANSO in one portal and submit. Save the confirmation and check the document and reference status. Do not submit a second CAS-ANSO application as a fallback.

Frequently asked questions

Is CAS-ANSO the same as the Chinese Government Scholarship?

No. Choose CAS-ANSO in your chosen host’s portal and follow its deadline. The ANSO-CAS-TWAS/UNESCO award is a separate consideration within CAS-ANSO for eligible doctoral applicants from developing countries. If eligible, use the checkbox in that same application. Read TWAS eligibility.

Should I wait until my final diploma arrives?

Not necessarily. Both hosts describe provisional documentation for students on track to finish, but the dates and records differ as noted above. Ask your institution for a letter with the expected degree date and ask your host about any nonstandard transcript before the deadline.

Does the award pay for housing or all visa costs?

Do not budget on a free room or unlimited reimbursement. The call lists a tuition and application-fee waiver, stipend, health insurance, and conditional travel and visa support. Ask your host for an itemized housing estimate and confirm sponsor rules before booking travel.
