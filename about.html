About ScholarRadar

ScholarRadar is an independent educational platform created to make international academic opportunities easier to discover. We believe that access to information about education and research should not depend on a student's financial background or location.

Our mission

We help students, graduates and researchers identify scholarships, fellowships, internships, research positions and funded degree programs worldwide, with particular attention to opportunities in Asia and beyond.

What you can find here

  • Undergraduate, master's and doctoral funding opportunities.
  • Research internships, academic vacancies and fellowships.
  • Practical guidance on statements of purpose, study plans, academic CVs and contacting prospective supervisors.
  • Application planning tools and links to official providers.

Our editorial approach

We aim to prioritize official university, government and funding organization sources. We summarize opportunities in our own words and provide links so readers can confirm eligibility, funding and deadlines. We welcome corrections because information may change after publication.

Free and independent access

ScholarRadar does not charge visitors to browse or require registration. We are not an admissions agency, university or scholarship awarding body. We do not guarantee admission or funding. Official providers may independently charge application fees. Advertising may help support the site.

Help us improve

Tell us about missing opportunities, errors or your experience through our contact and feedback page.
