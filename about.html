About ScholarRadar

ScholarRadar is an independent, free directory for international scholarships, internships and research opportunities. Our goal is to make academic opportunities easier to discover without requiring registration or charging visitors.

How we work

We link to original providers and distinguish directories from individual funding calls. Availability, eligibility and award terms can change; always confirm them with the official provider.

Independence

We do not award scholarships, process applications or guarantee admission. Third-party institutions may charge their own application fees.
