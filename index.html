Frequently asked questions

What does fully funded mean?

Coverage varies. Confirm tuition, living allowance, insurance and travel individually with the provider.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Some programs accept alternatives or waive testing. Check the specific admission rules.

Are all listings open?

No. Confirm dates on the official page; directories and undated opportunities are not confirmed open.

Does this website guarantee admission?

No. ScholarRadar is an independent information resource.

Application toolkit

How to contact a potential supervisor

Introduce your current degree and research interests, explain the connection to one specific project or paper, and ask whether funded openings are available. Attach a concise CV if appropriate.

Subject: Prospective graduate researcher — [research topic]
Dear Professor [Name],
I am [degree/background] with experience in [relevant skills]. Your work on [specific topic] aligns with my interest in [research question]. Are you considering graduate researchers for [intake]? I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss potential fit.
Kind regards,
[Name]
Prepare for a scholarship interview

Practice explaining your research, motivation, program fit, contributions, career objectives and funding needs. Use specific examples and prepare questions for the panel.
