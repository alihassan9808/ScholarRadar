GUIDE 01

Statement of Purpose

Explain your preparation, research interests and fit.

Read guide & sample

Start with a focused academic motivation. Describe relevant projects, your own contributions and outcomes. Connect your future interests to specific faculty and close with career goals. Follow the program’s word limit.

Illustrative ScholarRadar sample

My interest in sustainable energy grew while investigating why material composition affects device performance. In my final-year project, I compared candidate materials and analyzed the resulting trends. Graduate study would allow me to develop stronger experimental and analytical skills. At [program], I hope to investigate [specific question] with researchers working on [verified topic]. My long-term goal is to contribute to reliable energy technologies.

GUIDE 02

Study Plan & Research Proposal

Turn your goals into a feasible academic roadmap.

Read guide & sample

Explain your background, proposed topic, research gap, objectives, methods, timeline, university fit and expected outcomes. For taught degrees, emphasize modules and skills instead of inventing a thesis. Cite the literature and check feasibility.

Illustrative ScholarRadar sample

Title: Evaluating sustainable energy materials
Objective: Identify how composition influences performance.
Months 1–3: Review literature and complete relevant training.
Months 4–8: Design and conduct comparative experiments.
Months 9–12: Analyze results and prepare a dissertation.
Expected outcome: Evidence-based recommendations and a reproducible dataset. Adapt the timeline and methods to the actual program.

GUIDE 03

Academic CV

Present your qualifications with clear evidence.

Read guide & sample

Use reverse chronological education and experience. Include research projects, publications with accurate status, technical skills, awards and relevant teaching. Quantify contributions where possible. Use consistent dates and follow program-specific length requirements.

Illustrative ScholarRadar sample

[Full name] | [Professional email] | [Research profile]
EDUCATION
[Degree], [University], [Dates]
RESEARCH EXPERIENCE
[Project], [Institution], [Dates]
• Investigated [question] using [method]; reported [actual result].
PUBLICATIONS
[Accurate citation and publication status]
SKILLS
[Relevant methods and software]
AWARDS & REFERENCES
[Verified awards; referees if requested]

Frequently asked questions

What does fully funded mean?

Coverage varies. Confirm tuition, living allowance, insurance and travel individually with the provider.

Can I apply without IELTS?

Some programs accept alternatives or waive testing. Check the specific admission rules.

Are all listings open?

No. Confirm dates on the official page; directories and undated opportunities are not confirmed open.

Does this website guarantee admission?

No. ScholarRadar is an independent information resource.

Application toolkit

How to contact a potential supervisor

Introduce your current degree and research interests, explain the connection to one specific project or paper, and ask whether funded openings are available. Attach a concise CV if appropriate.

Subject: Prospective graduate researcher — [research topic]
Dear Professor [Name],
I am [degree/background] with experience in [relevant skills]. Your work on [specific topic] aligns with my interest in [research question]. Are you considering graduate researchers for [intake]? I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss potential fit.
Kind regards,
[Name]
Prepare for a scholarship interview

Practice explaining your research, motivation, program fit, contributions, career objectives and funding needs. Use specific examples and prepare questions for the panel.
