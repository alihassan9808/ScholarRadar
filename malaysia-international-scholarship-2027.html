Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS): 2027 status and application guide

Official information reviewed: 22 November 2026. This page is an independent guide, not a scholarship application or an announcement of a new call.

Applications are not currently confirmed open. Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education states that applications for the 2026/2027 academic year have closed. Its portal lists an award-status checking period from 8 October to 31 December 2026. A separate 2027/2028 application window has not been verified.

What is MIS?

The Malaysia International Scholarship is a Malaysian government programme for eligible international postgraduate students studying in Malaysia. The 2026 guidelines cover full-time master's and doctoral studies, including eligible continuing students. The next intake may have different rules: do not assume that the 2026 terms apply to a future call.

Eligibility and restrictions to verify

The 2026 official guidelines identify designated recipient countries, eligible universities and postgraduate programmes. Citizenship eligibility is not universal. Before preparing an application, check the country list, age limits, academic results, English-language evidence, study mode, course restrictions and continuing-student conditions in the official 2026 guidelines (PDF). No language-test waiver should be assumed without an explicit current policy.

Funding, dates and fees

The exact funding package, tuition coverage, stipend, duration, start date, application charges and any travel or insurance benefits for a future 2027/2028 call are not yet verified. Do not budget using third-party figures. Confirm each amount and condition in the new official call once published. The 2026/2027 application period is closed; there is no verified future application deadline or timezone to report.

Documents to prepare while waiting

Prepare your passport, degree certificates and transcripts, academic CV, research or study plan, evidence of language proficiency, and referee contacts. These are preparation suggestions, not a confirmed document checklist for the next MIS call. Review the official portal's current forms and instructions before submitting anything. Do not send personal documents to ScholarRadar.

How to apply when the next call opens

  1. Read the new call on the Ministry's official MIS portal and confirm that applications are open.
  2. Check your nationality, institution, programme, academic qualifications, language evidence and any age rules.
  3. Confirm the official funding and document requirements and prepare the prescribed forms.
  4. Submit only through the official application system before the published deadline; retain the confirmation.
  5. Use the official status-checking service for decisions and follow-up.
