Taiwan Graduate Admissions and Scholarships 2027: NTU, NTHU and TIGP

Official sources checked 28 November 2026. All deadlines below use Taiwan time (UTC+8). Admission and funding decisions are separate unless the provider says otherwise.

Choose your application route

  • National Taiwan University (NTU): September 2027 entry, second round opens 1 December at 11:00 and ends 28 January at 16:00. Check whether your programme is in this round.
  • National Tsing Hua University (NTHU): international graduate applications from 15 December to 25 February at 15:00. Start referee and bank documents early.
  • Academia Sinica TIGP: PhD only, no application fee. Deadline 1 February; the application portal had a reported outage.

National Taiwan University: September 2027

Round two runs 1 December 2026 to 28 January 2027. The first-time fee is NT$2,000 or US$80 for each of the first two choices, not for both together. Check the department's language, referee and document rules before paying. Applicants completing the qualifying degree can check NTU’s provisional graduation or enrollment certificate option. Round-two admission and scholarship decisions are scheduled for 22 April 2027.

Applicants from participating partner universities should check the Centennial Fellowship separately. For example, Chulalongkorn University lists 19 January 2027 for its second round. Do not assume the general 28 January deadline applies to that nomination. Chula lists NT$300,000 per year for master's and NT$500,000 per year for doctoral fellows; verify fee coverage and payment timing before budgeting.

National Tsing Hua University: Fall 2027

Applications open 15 December 2026 and close 25 February 2027 at 15:00. The handbook lists a nonrefundable NT$2,000 total for one or two programmes and NT$4,000 total for three or four, with a published nationality exemption for applicants from eligible least developed countries. Both recommendations must be complete before final submission.

Prepare official financial evidence issued within three months (at least NT$120,000 or US$4,000), or evidence of a scholarship you have already been awarded. Applying for an NTHU scholarship is not proof of funds. If a sponsor provides the balance statement, check the signed responsibility form and how to document the relationship. If your degree is pending, check the handbook's expected-graduation certificate requirements.

Request scholarship consideration in the admission application. Category A includes living support and fee coverage; Category B offers fee coverage only. Check which fees and benefits are stated in your award.

Academia Sinica TIGP: Fall 2027 PhD

The Fall 2027 call covers 14 doctoral tracks. Applications are scheduled through 1 February 2027 and there is no application fee. Choose one TIGP programme and follow its language, transcript and recommendation instructions.

Portal status: Academia Sinica reported a technical outage on 27 November. Access could not be confirmed on 28 November. Check the official notice and retry the portal before the published deadline; no extension has been verified.

TIGP lists a NT$40,000 monthly first-year stipend, with a performance-based second-year extension. Later support may depend on the programme and adviser. Partner-university tuition is separate; confirm tuition, subsidy and funding by year.

Before submitting

  1. Confirm your programme accepts your degree status and language evidence.
  2. Have your referees ready before opening the application. Check whether their letters must arrive before you can submit.
  3. Separate application fees, tuition waivers, monthly stipend and financial proof. Use the ScholarRadar funding calculator to estimate your budget.
