Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC) — China

Independent application guide · Sources reviewed 27 November 2026

Application status

2027 deadlines depend on the university or nominating authority; no universal closing date verified.

Funding and benefits

Undergraduate and graduate routes vary by call. Full awards typically include tuition, accommodation support, stipend and medical insurance; confirm exact coverage.

Eligibility and application route

Choose the embassy or designated nominating authority route (Type A), or a participating university route (Type B), according to the current call. For Type B, obtain the university agency number and confirm whether a separate university application is required.

Two 2027 routes compared

Pakistan HEC: Type A

The HEC portal closes 5 January 2027 at 4:00 pm Pakistan time (11:00 UTC). Apply through HEC and the CSC portal using agency 5861. HEC lists 61 places, including 15 for PhD applicants. It prefers a university pre-admission letter for master's and PhD applicants but does not list it as mandatory in its document checklist. Check the HAT score and test-date rules for graduates separately from undergraduate CSCA requirements.

HEC application steps · HEC document checklist

Xiamen University: Type B High-Level Postgraduate

Applications run from 1 December 2026 to 20 February 2027. Submit on both portals, select Type B and use agency 10384 in CSC. For graduate applicants, the pre-acceptance letter must be signed by an XMU supervisor, stamped by the college and uploaded to both systems. The published award covers tuition, housing or an accommodation subsidy, and medical insurance. The listed monthly stipends are CNY 3,000 for master's students and CNY 3,500 for doctoral students. XMU's doctoral admission guide lists a nonrefundable CNY 400 application fee.

Document preparation

Prepare passport, academic certificates and transcripts, language evidence, study plan, references where required, and any medical or police clearance specified by the call. Confirm whether a supervisor letter is mandatory.
