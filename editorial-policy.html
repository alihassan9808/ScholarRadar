Editorial and corrections policy

ScholarRadar is an independent discovery and application-planning resource. It is not a scholarship provider, university admissions office or immigration authority.

How to interpret our listings

Our editorial standard is to link to the original provider, distinguish active calls from general funding directories, and avoid presenting an unconfirmed deadline as an open application. Existing entries may not yet meet every standard; confirm the current intake, eligibility, funding coverage and deadline on the official page before applying.

When a date is unknown it should be marked unconfirmed rather than estimated. Funding should be described as fully funded only when the provider's terms support that description. A tuition discount is not equivalent to a living stipend.

Verification and corrections

To report an inaccurate listing, provide the page title, the disputed detail and the official source URL. Do not include passport numbers, login credentials or private application files. Use our contact page. If submission is unavailable, do not assume the report was delivered.

Corrections should distinguish a provider's changed requirements from an original publishing error. The provider's current instructions take precedence over our summaries.

Independence and safe applications

ScholarRadar does not charge application fees or guarantee admission. Always follow official payment instructions and avoid intermediaries promising guaranteed scholarships. Advertising, if enabled, must be distinct from editorial listings and does not constitute an endorsement.

Our free calculators and planning guides are informational tools, not eligibility decisions. Check individual requirements and seek clarification directly from the funding provider.
