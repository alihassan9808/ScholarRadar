KAUST Fellowship 2027: funded graduate study in Saudi Arabia

Open Fall 2027 round — verified 27 November 2026. King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), Thuwal, offers graduate study in science and engineering. The official application portal confirms that MS, MS/PhD and PhD applications are open.

Dates and funding

Round 2 opened 28 September 2026 and is scheduled to close 3 January 2027. The official timeline does not specify a cutoff hour or timezone; submit early. Decisions are scheduled for 25 February and teaching begins 29 August 2027. Dates may change. Spring admission is closed according to the application portal.

The university describes its fellowship as covering tuition, living support equivalent to USD 20,000–30,000 annually, accommodation, medical and dental benefits and relocation assistance. Support extends through graduate study; confirm the exact duration and award conditions in your offer. Dependent benefits may involve charges.

Eligibility and application preparation

Choose a relevant graduate program and confirm its degree prerequisites. The university's general applying page lists IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL iBT 81, with an exemption for degrees from accredited institutions in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand. GRE is not compulsory. Confirm current program-specific requirements before applying. Nationality restrictions, age limits, minimum GPA, exact degree duration and application fee were not established from the sources checked for this article.

Prepare your CV, academic records, English evidence where applicable and academic references. The university requires referees to submit their own recommendations through the application. Check the portal for the complete document list and any program-specific statement requirements.

  1. Review the graduate program and identify research groups that match your experience.
  2. Read the official timeline and prepare the required records and references.
  3. Submit through the official application portal and monitor requests for additional information.

For research applicants, make a short evidence map linking each relevant project to a research question, your contribution and a skill you can bring to the laboratory. This is an independent preparation suggestion, not an additional university requirement.

Frequently asked questions

Is Spring 2027 still open?

No, the application portal states it is closed. Use the Fall round instead.

Is IELTS always required?

No. TOEFL is an alternative and the university lists specific degree-location exemptions. Do not assume an English-medium letter alone qualifies.

How should I compare funding?

Check the actual stipend, dependent costs and housing terms in the offer. Use our funding comparison guide to estimate your budget.
