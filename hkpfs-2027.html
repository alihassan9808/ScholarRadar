Hong Kong PhD Fellowship 2027/28

Last checked: 26 November 2026. The fellowship is open to applicants worldwide seeking a new full-time PhD. The Research Grants Council (RGC) lists an annual stipend of HK$344,400 and a separate annual conference and research travel allowance of HK$14,400 for up to three years. Universities may add support. Before choosing, check what happens after year three and whether tuition and housing are actually paid.

Two submissions on 1 December 2026

  1. 12:00 noon Hong Kong time (11:00 Bangkok, 04:00 UTC): Submit the free RGC initial application and save the reference number from the confirmation email. Prepare your program choices before opening the form; it can time out after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  2. 23:59 Hong Kong time (22:59 Bangkok, 15:59 UTC): Submit the full application for each selected program, using the same RGC reference number. The RGC deadline table lists this cutoff for all eight universities this year. Check each program’s documents, fees and referee instructions before submitting the RGC form.

Put your preferred university first

One RGC record can hold up to two programs at one or two universities. If both universities nominate you, the RGC processes the nomination from your higher-ranked university. Within one university, the university decides which program, if any, to nominate you for. You can change your choices in the original record until noon Hong Kong time. A duplicate initial application can disqualify you, and declining the first-choice fellowship does not transfer it to your second choice. Read the RGC application rules.

Compare what you would pay

  • HKU: HK$780 application fee. Its 2027/28 fellowship package lists a tuition waiver for the normal study period and a guaranteed first-year hall place.
  • PolyU: HK$600 application fee for 2027/28. Its published package lists a tuition waiver for the normal study period and a guaranteed accommodation place for two years.
  • HKUST: HK$180 for Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering. The HKUST award page advertises a four-year tuition waiver for fellows, subject to renewal. A separate first-year waiver is listed for some eligible candidates admitted without the fellowship.

A guaranteed room is not necessarily a free room. Check the written offer before assuming rent, tuition or a fourth year is covered. Use the ScholarRadar funding calculator to compare living costs and how much cash you need before the first stipend.

Check extra forms and backup routes

Disclose relevant AI use to the university under RGC and university rules, including editing or literature searches if applicable. Keep a short record of which tools you used, why, how extensively, and what you verified yourself. HKUST requires a supplementary AI and data-management form and lists 8 December 2026 for referees.

Regular PhD admission is separate. HKUST Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering lists 1 June 2027 for non-local full-time applicants on its regular route, and HKU lists clearing rounds subject to vacancies. Neither extends the fellowship deadline or guarantees funding.
