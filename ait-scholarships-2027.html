AIT Scholarships 2027

Official sources checked: 25 November 2026. AIT publishes different dates on different pages; see the notes below before applying.

Destination
Thailand
Level
Master’s and PhD
Tuition scholarship deadlines
The award page lists 10 December 2026 for January 2027 and 15 July 2027 for August 2027. The online application calendar lists 10 June for August AIT Scholarships and 30 June for overseas applicants who need an education visa. For August funding, aim for 10 June unless AIT confirms a later date for your application.
Funding
Full or partial tuition waiver; registration THB 20,000 per semester and living costs around THB 11,500–15,000 monthly are the student’s responsibility.
Eligibility
All nationalities; recognized four-year bachelor’s degree; PhD applicants need a master’s, GPA 3.50 at master’s and 2.75 undergraduate from top universities. The tuition award page lists IELTS Academic or AIT EET at 5.0 for master’s and 5.5 for doctoral applicants. Those figures do not replace degree admission criteria. For standard doctoral admission, AIT lists IELTS Academic 6 overall and 6 in writing, or AIT EET 6, and requires two recommendation letters and a 5–10 page research proposal.
Application and documents
Apply through AIT admission; explain funding request in SOP. Other documents and fees: consult admission requirements.
Other details
Age limit, duration, fees or start date not independently confirmed unless stated above.

Separate award: His Majesty the King’s Doctoral Scholarship

This Royal Thai Government scholarship is for qualified applicants from Asian countries in participating doctoral programmes. It covers tuition, registration, campus accommodation and a living bursary for 41 months. AIT lists a four-year bachelor’s and two-year master’s, each with a GPA of at least 3.5 or equivalent, IELTS 6 overall and in writing or AIT EET 6, two recommendations and a research proposal.

Deadline discrepancy: The August 2027 award page and online application calendar list 31 March 2027, but the general admissions page lists 28 February for the King’s awards. Aim for the earlier date unless AIT confirms otherwise. Submit through AIT’s online admission portal and indicate the scholarship in your statement of purpose.
