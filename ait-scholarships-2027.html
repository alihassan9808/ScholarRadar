AIT Scholarships 2027

Official call checked: 19 November 2026. Confirm changes with provider before applying.

Destination
Thailand
Level
Master’s and PhD
Deadline
10 December 2026 (January intake); 15 July 2027 (August intake)
Funding
Full or partial tuition waiver; registration THB 20,000 per semester and living costs around THB 11,500–15,000 monthly are the student’s responsibility.
Eligibility
All nationalities; recognized four-year bachelor’s degree; PhD applicants need a master’s, GPA 3.50 at master’s and 2.75 undergraduate from top universities. IELTS Academic or AIT EET minimum 5.0 master’s / 5.5 PhD.
Application and documents
Apply through AIT admission; explain funding request in SOP. Other documents and fees: consult admission requirements.
Other details
Age limit, duration, fees or start date not independently confirmed unless stated above.
