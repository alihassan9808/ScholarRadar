Chula LINK Scholarship — Thailand

Independent application guide · Sources reviewed 27 November 2026

Application status

The second-semester 2026 call closed on 31 October 2026. The next call is not verified.

Funding and benefits

Tuition support, monthly living allowance and round-trip airfare are listed in the official 2026 call. Confirm amounts and exclusions in the next announcement.

Eligibility and application route

International applicants without a Thai passport admitted to a master's or doctoral program were eligible under the 2026 call; the second-semester call also allowed eligible first-semester students. Contact your program coordinator and follow the new call's application route.

Document preparation

Prepare admission evidence, passport, transcripts and any documents specified in the new call. Do not reuse the closed application form.
