OIST Research Internship: Fall 2027 in Japan

Official sources checked 28 November 2026. Next round announced; opening date not independently confirmed.

Dates and funding

Deadline
15 April 2027, 23:59 Japan time (UTC+9). Spring 2027 applications closed on 15 October 2026.
Placement
Four to six months between October 2027 and March 2028; results expected mid-June 2027. Dates may change.
Support
JPY 2,400 per working day, furnished housing, return airfare, shuttle access and visa paperwork assistance. Support is for the intern only.
Costs
Nonrefundable JPY 5,000 application fee. Tuition is not applicable to this non-degree placement; other personal expenses should be budgeted.

Who should apply?

Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology welcomes suitable bachelor's students in their final two years by placement start, master's students and graduates of either degree from institutions in Japan or overseas. PhD students and PhD graduates are excluded. Current students need institutional permission. No numerical GPA or age cutoff is specified on the reviewed application page.

Projects span STEM, including chemistry, physics, computational science, marine research and neuroscience. Match your existing skills to an available host project rather than selecting solely by subject name.

Prepare your application

Gather academic records, degree or enrollment evidence, CV, identity document and recent photo. Draft a statement of purpose within 250 words and 1,500 characters explaining your research objective and career fit. Arrange one to three faculty recommendations; at least one must arrive before submission. English-language documents are required; IELTS/TOEFL scores are optional.

Check the official faculty and project table before paying the fee. As of 28 November 2026, its latest column covers April–September 2027 starts, not Fall 2027. Recheck for the October 2027–March 2028 period before choosing your hosts. Submit through the official portal; OIST asks applicants not to seek faculty approval or support in advance. A listed project does not guarantee a placement.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply without IELTS?

Yes, test scores are optional for this internship; this is not a general waiver for OIST degree programs.

Is the application free?

No. Budget the application fee before submitting and confirm current payment instructions.

How can I improve my application?

Identify a feasible research question, explain what technique you want to learn and give evidence of your preparation. Use the ScholarRadar writing resources to organize your documents.
