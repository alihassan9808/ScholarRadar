Privacy Policy

Effective and updated: 21 November 2026.

This policy explains how ScholarRadar handles information when you browse our educational directory or contact us.

1. Information we receive

No registration is needed. When you email us, we receive your email address and any information you voluntarily include, such as your name, rating or feedback. Please do not send passports, financial information or confidential application documents.

2. Technical information and analytics

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may process IP addresses, request details, browser information and other technical data to deliver and protect the website. Cloudflare Web Analytics helps us understand aggregate visits and performance. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

3. Local storage and cookies

Saved listings, checklist progress and application planning entries are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes these entries. Your browser provides controls for cookies and site storage; disabling storage may affect these tools.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

ScholarRadar includes Google AdSense code. When advertising is served, Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies or similar identifiers to select and measure advertisements, including personalized ads informed by previous visits to this and other websites. Google and its advertising partners may process device and usage information for these purposes.

Manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center or industry opt-out controls. Read how Google processes information from partner websites and Google's privacy policy. Where required, appropriate consent controls must be in place before ads are served.

5. How information is used and shared

Technical and voluntarily submitted information may be used to operate and secure the site, understand usage, respond to messages, correct listings and improve resources. Hosting, advertising and email providers process information under their own applicable policies. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

6. Feedback and public reviews

Feedback is sent through your email application. Reviews and chosen display names may be published after moderation only if you give permission. Your email address is not intended for publication. You can request removal of a published review.

7. Retention and security

Browser-stored information remains until removed by you or your browser. Email correspondence should be retained only as reasonably needed for responding, moderation, security or legal obligations. No internet transmission is completely secure.

8. External websites and international processing

Scholarship providers operate independently and have their own privacy policies. Hosting, advertising and email providers may process information outside your country. Review their terms before submitting applications.

9. Children and privacy choices

Our educational listings are publicly accessible, but children under 13 should not submit personal information. Parents or guardians may contact us about information submitted by a child. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied, or withdraw permission to publish a review.

10. Updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date. For questions or privacy requests, email ateeqleghari@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
